- Absolute Cell References

## 3. Excel Formulas Practised

- `COUNTA()` – Counted non-empty job records.
- `COUNTIF()` – Counted job postings matching a particular skill.
- `COUNTIFS()` – Counted records based on multiple conditions.
- `SUM()` – Calculated totals.
- `AVERAGE()` – Calculated averages.
- `MIN()` and `MAX()` – Identified minimum and maximum values.

## 4. Cell References Practice

Practised three types of cell references:

- Relative Reference: `A1`
- Absolute Reference: `$A$1`
- Mixed Reference: `$A1` and `A$1`

Used an absolute reference to calculate skill percentages and mixed references to create a calculation table.

Example formula:

`=$T21*U$20`

## 5. Key Learnings

- Learned how to organize and analyse job-market data in Excel.
- Understood how to count job postings based on specific conditions.
- Practised calculating percentages using absolute references.
- Understood how relative, absolute and mixed references behave when copying formulas.
- Gained practical experience working with a real-world-style dataset.
